Research

When should humans intervene in AI decisions?

My current research interest is the design and evaluation of human oversight in increasingly autonomous AI systems.

Work in progress · 2026

Comparing proactive and reactive human oversight

I'm studying the trade-offs between intervening before an autonomous system acts and reviewing decisions after the fact. The questions include trust, user control, perceived responsibility, and when human involvement is useful rather than merely ceremonial.

Research question: How does the timing of human intervention affect people's experience of AI-supported decisions?
Related implementation: TrustFlow explores practical review queues, policy rules, overrides, and auditability.
Status: Ongoing research work. Not presented here as published or experimentally validated results.
